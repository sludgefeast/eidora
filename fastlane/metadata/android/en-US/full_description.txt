Eidora finds and groups faces in your photos — entirely on your device. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking. Your photos never leave your phone.

Features:
* On-device face detection and recognition
* Groups photos of the same person automatically
* Writes recognised names back into your photo files as XMP metadata, compatible with DigiKam and Aves
* You choose which folders are analysed
* Battery- and temperature-aware background processing you can pause and resume
* Fully offline

The machine learning models are downloaded once from the project's releases after you confirm. The default models (YuNet and SFace) are free Apache-2.0 licensed. Optional higher-accuracy models are available for non-commercial research use; you choose in Settings, where each model's license is shown (see the repository for details).

Eidora is free software (GPLv3).
