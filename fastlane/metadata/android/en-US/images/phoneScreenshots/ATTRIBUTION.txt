Screenshot image sources
=========================

The photographs shown in these store screenshots are historical portraits
taken from Wikimedia Commons, each marked there as public domain (PD-old /
PD-US / Library of Congress "no known restrictions on publication"). They are
not original work by the Eidora project and are used only in a neutral,
technical/editorial context (demonstrating on-device face detection and
recognition), not for advertising.

People depicted in the screenshots: Albert Einstein (d. 1955), Marie Curie
(d. 1934), Pierre Curie (d. 1906).

Each file is fetched from:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/<filename>
and its Commons page (with author, date and licence) is:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<filename>

The full per-photo list — filename, year and public-domain basis — is kept in
ATTRIBUTION.md at the repository root. That file is the authoritative record;
verify each photo's status on its linked Commons page before relying on it.

Not legal advice — this is a good-faith record.
